Our Story

Rooted in the Sahyadris, Driven by Seva

Sevak Sahyadriche Foundation was founded by a small group of trekkers and heritage enthusiasts in Pune who wanted trekking to mean more than reaching a summit. What began as weekend fort visits among friends has grown into a foundation that organizes treks, heritage walks, and cultural events across Maharashtra — always anchored in respect for history, community, and the mountains themselves.

Every trek we run pairs the physical climb with the story of the fort: the Maratha history, the local communities living around it, and the traditions that are still kept alive today. We also run cultural events — from Gudi Padwa celebrations to Lavani and Powada evenings — to celebrate Maharashtrian heritage beyond the trail.
