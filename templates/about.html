Our Story

Rooted in the Sahyadris.
Driven by Seva.

We believe trekking is more than reaching a summit. It is a way to connect with Maharashtra's mountains, history, people and living culture.

Who We Are

From weekend treks to a shared purpose.

Sevak Sahyadriche Foundation was founded by a small group of trekkers and heritage enthusiasts in Pune who wanted trekking to mean more than simply reaching a summit.

What began as weekend fort visits among friends has grown into a foundation organizing treks, heritage walks and cultural events across Maharashtra.

Every journey is rooted in respect for history, local communities and the mountains themselves.

What We Stand For

Our work is guided by four simple values.

These principles shape how we trek, how we work with communities and how we preserve the heritage around us.

Preserve Heritage

Documenting and sharing the history of Sahyadri forts and the Maratha legacy with every generation.

Promote Safe Trekking

Trained trek leaders, safety-first planning and routes suited to different experience levels.

Celebrate Culture

Keeping Maharashtrian folk arts, festivals and traditions alive through community events.

Give Back

Supporting local guides, porters and communities around the places we visit.
